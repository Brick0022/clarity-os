KEEP YOUR WORK WITH YOU

Your data, in your hands.

A little housekeeping for your personal workspace.

Backup & restore

Export your tasks, goals, focus history and current decision as a JSON backup. Import it to restore your workspace or move it to another browser.

Import replaces the current workspace after you confirm. Only ClarityOS v1 backups from this app are supported.

How saving works

ClarityOS stores your information in this browser using localStorage. There are no accounts, cloud sync, analytics or external services. Your information is not uploaded by this app.

Another device, browser or website address has its own separate workspace. Clearing browser data can remove your work, and private browsing may not keep it. Export backups regularly. If you open this HTML directly, storage behaviour depends on your browser; use your published website for a consistent workspace.
